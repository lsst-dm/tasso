Using tasso

The tasso main page presents a triplet of images. Use the buttons to classify the source. You can also use keyboard shortcuts. After submitting your classification, a new subject will appear.

Classification Guide

Real: Transient

Positive point source with no point source in the template image.

Examples: 1, 2

Real: Variable

Positive or negative point source with corresponding point sources in the science and template image.

Examples: 1, 2, 3

Real: Trailed Source

PSF-width extended streak; only in the science and difference image.

Examples: 1

Noise/no detection

No clear excess in the center of the difference image.

Examples: 1, 2, 3

Unsure/ambiguous

General class for cases where the labeler is unsure.

Dipole

Clear positive and negative lobes in the difference image.

Examples: 1, 2, 3

General Bogus

General class for artifacts.

Examples: 1, 2, 3

Bad Subtraction

Image differencing problems: mixed residuals of positive and negative pixels.

Examples: 1, 2, 3

Bright Star/Diffraction Spike

Detection on the wings or diffraction spikes of a bright star.

Examples: 1, 2, 3

Cosmic Ray

Narrow, non-PSF positive excess; seen in both difference and science image.

Examples: 1, 2, 3
